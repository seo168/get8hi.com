Payment and account checks

8HI Payment Guide 2026 | Deposit, Withdrawal & UPI

Payment details must match the account and instructions visible in the official app. Never send money to a personal contact claiming to accelerate a deposit or withdrawal.

Published by Get 8HI · Updated 28 August 2026
01

Before making a deposit

Confirm account details, amount, payment reference and the destination displayed in the verified application.

02

Withdrawal checks and delays

Review account verification, submitted details, status messages and stated processing requirements.

03

First deposit rebate

The listed levels are 2% with no star, 3% at one star, 4% at two stars and 5% at three stars.

04

Agent turnover commission

Referral levels are listed as 0.2%, 0.3%, 0.45% and 0.6% from Downline 1 through Downline 4.

05

Deposit instruction checklist

Read the amount, destination, payment reference and expiry information before confirming a transfer. Use the details displayed for the current transaction rather than a saved destination from an older session. Keep the receipt and avoid submitting the same payment repeatedly while an earlier attempt is pending.

06

UPI and QR verification

Confirm the payee information shown by the payment app before authorising a UPI or QR request. A collect request is not the same as receiving money. Never enter a UPI PIN to receive a withdrawal, and reject unexpected requests that do not match an action started in the verified account.

07

Withdrawal review process

Check account verification, name consistency, payment destination and any stated processing window. Record the request identifier and status. A pending withdrawal should be investigated through the official support route; paying a personal contact cannot legitimately accelerate platform processing.

08

Reward qualification example

For the downline reward, the invited person must deposit ₹200 and finish ₹600 turnover. The agent amount for W5W-equivalent participation is ₹100, the mentor component is ₹50 through Telegram, and the qualifying downline amount is ₹20. Eligibility should be checked before assuming payment is automatic.

09

Rebate and commission calculation notes

Percentages apply to the qualifying base defined by the program, not necessarily to every visible account movement. First-deposit rebate uses the 2% to 5% star table. Referral turnover commission follows 0.2%, 0.3%, 0.45% and 0.6% levels; records should identify the correct downline relationship.

10

Transaction reconciliation

Group B users should reconcile the amount, reference and final status across the app and payment provider. A successful bank-side message does not by itself identify the correct platform account, while a pending app status does not justify paying the same instruction twice.
