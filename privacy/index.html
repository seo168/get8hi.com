8HICOM Privacy Policy 2026

This policy describes the current informational website. It will be updated before any new account or payment data processing is introduced.

Published by Get 8HI · Updated 28 August 2026
01

Information processed

Hosting and security systems may process standard request data.

02

Cookies and local storage

Essential technology may support reliable and secure operation.

03

External links and downloads

Review the destination policy before using a third-party service.

04

Your choices

Browser controls may be used to manage non-essential storage where available.

05

Server and security records

Web infrastructure may process IP-related signals, user agent, requested path, timestamp and security events to deliver and protect the service. Retention should be limited to operational, fraud-prevention and legal needs, with access restricted to authorised service providers.

06

Account information boundaries

The current informational website should not imply that it collects active app-account or payment data when those functions are unavailable. Before such processing begins, the policy must identify data categories, purposes, retention, sharing and user-request procedures.

07

Download and external destination data

Opening an external service or downloading through delivery infrastructure can expose technical request information to that provider. Visitors should review the destination and avoid uploading identity or financial information through an unverified form.

08

Cookies and measurement

Essential storage may support security and reliable navigation. Non-essential analytics or advertising technology should be documented before activation and controlled where required. Browser settings can remove stored data, although blocking essential functions may affect site operation.

09

Security and user requests

A published contact route should explain how to request access, correction or deletion where applicable. Never send passwords, verification codes or full payment credentials in a general privacy message. Identity verification for a request should be proportionate to the data involved.

10

Group B domain separation

Technical logs and policies belong to the domain that receives the request. A privacy statement on get8hi.com does not automatically cover an external partner, messaging account or unrelated support domain.
